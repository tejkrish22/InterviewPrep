> [!note]+ ### Questions
> ### 1. Opportunity Context & Role Clarity
> - Why is this role not full-time within Caterpillar Inc.?
> - What problem/project is this role solving?
> - What exactly is the **project scope**?
> - Has this project existed before? If yes, how long has it been running?
> - How many contractors were hired previously and how long did they stay?
> - Is this a **long-term funded program or a pilot/experimental initiative?**
> 
> ---
> ### 2. Role & Reporting Structure
> - Whom will I report to in Caterpillar Inc. org chart?
> - Will I be treated as part of the internal team or as external/vendor?
> - Will I have access to internal tools, codebases, and meetings?
> - Who will evaluate my performance—vendor or Caterpillar manager?
> 
> ---
> ### 3. Work Nature & Expectations
> - What would a typical **week look like**?
> - Expected working hours (India time vs US overlap?)
> - Any on-call expectations?
> - Performance evaluation criteria?
> - What time will you spend on this project?
> - Are there strict deadlines or flexible delivery expectations?
> 
> ---
> ### 4. Work Setup & Infrastructure
> - Who provides the **laptop and equipment**?
> - Will it be a **secure Caterpillar system or vendor system**?
> - Work mode:
>     - Fully remote / Hybrid / Office
> - If office required:
>     - Is office already set up?
>     - Who handles infra setup?
> 
> ---
> ### 5. Compensation & Payment Risk
> - Fixed vs variable components breakdown
> - Is the **sign-on bonus conditional** (clawback clause?)
> - What is the **salary payment cycle**?
> - Have there been **delays in salary historically**?
> - Who processes payroll?
> - Currency exposure: Is billing tied to USD?
> - Will I be paid during **bench/no-project periods**?
> - Is my salary **independent of project continuity**?
> 
> ---
> ### 6. Benefits & Policies
> - Health insurance (self + family?)
> - Accidental insurance
> - EPF contribution (yes/no)
> - Gratuity eligibility
> - Bonus eligibility (if any)
> 
> ---
> ### 7. Leaves & Holidays
> - Paid leaves
> - Sick leave
> - Public/government holidays (India or US calendar?)
> - Carry forward policy
> - Leave encashment
> 
> ---
> ### 8. Legal & Financial Structure
> - What is the **exact payment flow**?
>     - Caterpillar → Vendor → Me
> - Is there any **middle layer**?
> - Tax structure: Salary vs contract income?
> - Any **non-compete / non-solicit** clause?
> - Who is my **legal employer**?
> 
> ---
> ### 9. Contract & Job Security (CRITICAL)
> - What is the **contract duration**?
> - Is it **renewable automatically**?
> - What are the **early termination conditions**?
> - Who can terminate?
> - What is the **notice period (both sides)**?
> - What happens if Caterpillar ends the project abruptly?
> 
> ---
> ### 10. Growth & Conversion
> - Is there a possibility of **full-time conversion to Caterpillar US**?
> - Any examples of past conversions?
> - Annual hike policy (if contract extends)
> 
> ---
> ### 11. Exit & Future Safety
> - What happens if the **project ends suddenly**?
> - Will you provide **alternative projects**?
> - Can I transition internally?
> - Experience letter and exit documentation
> 
> ---
> ### 12. Transition Timeline
> - Hiring completion timeline of juniors as well.
> - Offer letter release timeline
> - Joining date
> - When should I resign from Caterpillar Inc.?
> - Background verification process?
> 
> ---
> ## 🔴 13. Founder / Startup Risk (NEW – CRITICAL)
> - Do you have **financial runway** to pay salaries if no contracts come?
> - For how many months can you sustain the team without revenue?
> - How many contracts are currently secured vs in pipeline?
> - What is your **plan if this project stops suddenly?**
> - How are you planning to scale this company in next 1–2 years?
> 
> ---
> ## 🟡 14. Equity / Stake (ONLY if discussed)
> - What % stake are you considering?
> - Will it be **legally documented (ESOP/equity agreement)?**
> - What is the **vesting schedule**?
> - What makes this equity valuable (profit share / exit / acquisition)?
> - When will this be formally defined?
> 
> ---
> ## ⚠️ 15. Direct Reality Questions (Ask calmly)
> - What are the **top 2 risks** in this setup?
> - What would make this fail?
> - What should I be cautious about before joining?
>
