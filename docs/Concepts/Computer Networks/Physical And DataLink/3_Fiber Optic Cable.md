>Transmits data as light signals. Light travels through a glass or plastic fiber.

## Why fiber is better than Copper cables?

1. Very high speed
2. Long distances
3. Low signal loss
4. High Bandwidth
5. Hard to tap physically

## Three layers of fiber cable

1. Core
	1. Innermost glass or plastic part
	2. Carries the light signal
2. Cladding
	1. Surrounds the core 
	2. Reflects the light back into the core
	3. Keeps lights travelling forward
3. Coating
	1. Outer protective plastic layer

## Types

1. Single Mode Fiber
	1. Very thin core
	2. Laser Light source
	3. Long distance
	4. High Performance
	5. Higher cost
2. Multi Mode Fiber
	1. Large Core
	2. LED Light source
	3. Shorter distance
	4. Good but limited bandwidth
	5. Cheaper
