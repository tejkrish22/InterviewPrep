> PAN -> LAN / WLAN -> CAN -> MAN -> WAN

## Personal Area Network (PAN)

1. Connected devices around one person like phone, earbuds and smart watch.
2. Very local, low power and personal use.
3. Typical Range - a few meters.

## Local Area Network (LAN)

1. Connects devices within a small area like a home or office or lab.
2. High speed, low latency and privately managed.

## Wireless LAN (WLAN)

1. A LAN that uses wifi instead of cables

## Campus Area Network (CAN)

1. Connects across multiple LANs across a campus, college or office park.

## Metropolitan Area Network (MAN)

1. Connects networks across a city or large urban area.
2. To connect multiple LANs across a city.
3. Larger than LAN, smaller than WAN.

## Wide Area Network (WAN)

1. Connects networks over very large distances.
2. Connect via fiber cables, undersea cables.
3. Ex: AWS, Internet itself.
