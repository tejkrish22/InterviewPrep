## Why ?

1. A powerful computer without a network is like a smart person locked alone in a room.
2. The real world needs machines to
	1. Exchange data
	2. Share files
	3. Serve users at scale
	4. Communicate across distance

## Network vs Internet

> Network does not always mean "Internet"

1. A network can be small
	1. Phone connected to earbuds
	2. Laptop connected to wifi
	3. Computers inside an office
	4. Servers inside a data centre
2. The Internet is the largest example of a network

Network ! = Internet
