>Describes how data is transmitted between devices.

## Half Duplex

>Only one direction active at a time.

1. Data can travel `both ways`.
2. But only `one side` at a time.
3. Collision possible
4. Lower performance

## Full Duplex

>Both direction active at a time

1. Data can travel `both ways`.
2. At the `same time`.
3. No Collision
4. Higher performance
