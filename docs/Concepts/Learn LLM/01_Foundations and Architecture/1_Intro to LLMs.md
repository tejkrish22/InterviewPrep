## Overview of Large Language Models

Large Language Models (LLMs) are deep learning models trained on vast text corpora to understand, synthesize, and generate human language. At a fundamental level, an LLM takes a sequence of tokens and predicts the most probable next token in the sequence.

---

## 3 Dimensions of LLM Engineering

Engineering applications around LLMs spans three primary dimensions:

1. **Models**:
   - Model selection: Choosing between closed-source and open-source models.
   - Model architectures: Mixture of Experts (MoE), dense architectures, multimodal capabilities (text, vision, audio).
2. **Tools**:
   - Frameworks and runtimes: Hugging Face, LangChain, LiteLLM, Ollama.
   - User interfaces and monitoring: Gradio, Weights & Biases, Modal.
3. **Techniques**:
   - Application integration: APIs, prompt engineering, structured outputs.
   - Context and behavior augmentation: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Fine-Tuning (PEFT/LoRA), and Agentic workflows.

## Model Landscape: Closed vs Open Source

### 1. Closed-Source Frontier Models

Proprietary foundation models accessed exclusively via commercial APIs or chat subscriptions:

- **OpenAI**: GPT series (`GPT-4o`, `GPT-4.1-mini`, `o1`, `o3-mini`).
- **Anthropic**: Claude series with tiered compute sizes:
  - *Haiku*: Ultra-fast, lightweight.
  - *Sonnet*: Balanced high-speed reasoning and coding.
  - *Opus*: Heavyweight complex reasoning.
- **Google**: Gemini series (`Gemini 1.5 Pro`, `Gemini 1.5 Flash`, `Gemini 2.0 Flash`).
- **xAI**: Grok series.

### 2. Open-Source and Open-Weight Models

Models where weights are publicly accessible and downloadable for self-hosting, fine-tuning, or local execution:

- **Meta**: Llama series (`Llama 2`, `Llama 3`, `Llama 3.2`, `Llama 3.3`). Pioneered widely accessible open-weight releases.
- **Mistral AI**: Mistral 7B and Mixtral 8x7B (pioneered open Mixture of Experts where multiple smaller sub-networks specialize on subsets of inputs).
- **Alibaba Cloud**: Qwen series (`Qwen 2.5`).
- **Microsoft**: Phi series (`Phi-3`, `Phi-4`), focusing on strong reasoning in small parameter footprints.
- **DeepSeek AI**: DeepSeek-V3 and DeepSeek-R1, proving state-of-the-art reasoning at dramatically lower pre-training and inference costs.

### Model Distillation

Distillation is the process of training a smaller model on synthetic training outputs or reasoning traces generated by a significantly larger teacher model. For example, smaller Llama or Qwen variants are distilled using reasoning outputs from larger frontier models, transferring reasoning capacity into low-latency, deployable sizes.

## The Three Flavors of LLMs

| Flavor | Primary Objective | Training & Behavior | Best Use Cases |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Base Model** | Raw Next-Token Prediction | Pre-trained on raw internet data without conversational alignment. Completes text sequences. | Starting point for custom fine-tuning and domain adaptation. |
| **Chat / Instruct Model** | Conversational Interaction | Post-trained using Instruction Fine-Tuning (SFT) and Reinforcement Learning (RLHF/DPO) to follow instructions. | Conversational assistants, document summarization, content drafting. |
| **Reasoning / Thinking Model** | Deliberative Problem Solving | Generates internal hidden thoughts or reasoning traces before returning final answers (e.g., DeepSeek-R1, OpenAI `o1`/`o3`). | Complex mathematics, algorithmic coding, logic puzzles, multi-step planning. |

### Reasoning Budget & Budget Forcing

- **Reasoning Budget**: The allowance of tokens an inference engine or model is allowed to expend thinking before generating the user-facing answer.
- **Budget Forcing**: Prompting techniques or system configurations that force the model to deliberate more deeply (e.g., instructing the model to question its initial assumptions or deliberately evaluate counter-arguments before answering).

## Strengths and Blind Spots of Frontier LLMs

### Core Strengths

- **Information Synthesis**: Condensing and cross-referencing complex concepts into clear, structured explanations.
- **Drafting & Fleshing Out**: Turning bullet points into structured prose, documentation, or specifications.
- **Software Engineering**: Writing boilerplate, refactoring, and debugging code under human supervision.

### Inherent Blind Spots

- **Hallucinations with High Confidence**: LLMs produce plausible-sounding but factually incorrect assertions with high conviction.
- **Training Cutoffs**: Knowledge is fixed at the training cut-off timestamp unless augmented with search or external tools.
- **Niche Domains**: Performance degrades on highly specialized PhD-level or rare domain tasks.
- **Counting & Precision Pitfalls**: Sub-word tokenization makes character-level counting (e.g., "how many 'a's are in this sentence?") tricky without tool assistance.
